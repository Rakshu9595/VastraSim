1. Introduction

FitMirror AI is a virtual fashion try-on interface. This page explains what information may be processed when you choose to use its features. Final privacy terms depend on the service configuration and require confirmation before production use.

2. How FitMirror AI works

You can submit a supported clothing product URL or upload a garment image, upload your own photograph, select a garment category, and request a visual try-on preview. The frontend is designed to communicate with a separate configured service. It does not contact the AI model directly.

3. Information you provide

Depending on how you use the interface, information may include a product URL, a garment image, a personal photograph, a garment category, and information returned about a try-on session. The service's actual collection and processing must be confirmed with its operators.

4. Photograph processing

Your personal photograph is sent only after you check the explicit consent control on the try-on page and request generation. A preview in your browser is local until you take that action. The frontend does not save personal photographs in local storage. The backend's processing and any subsequent handling are not verified here.

5. Product URL processing

If you request product extraction, the product URL is sent to the configured application service. Retailer support varies. Avoid submitting links that contain personal information or private access tokens.

6. Image storage

Uploaded images may be handled by the configured backend or storage provider to perform a requested try-on. Whether images are retained, where they are stored, and how they are protected must be confirmed before deployment. Do not rely on this interface as a statement of storage guarantees.

7. Try-on session history

The history page requests session data from the configured backend. It displays only data returned by that service and does not create sample saved sessions. Guest-session authorization and access controls depend on the backend implementation.

8. Data retention and deletion

No retention period is stated here because one has not been confirmed. Where a backend deletion endpoint is available, the interface can request deletion of a session. Confirm whether deleting a session also deletes associated images and backups.

9. Security practices

The frontend performs basic input and file checks and sends requests only to the configured application API. These checks do not replace server-side validation. Security controls, encryption, access permissions, and incident procedures must be documented by the service operator.

10. Third-party services

The intended architecture may involve product websites, an application backend, image storage, and an AI inference provider. The actual providers, their terms, and any transfer of data must be confirmed and disclosed before launch.

11. AI-generated image limitations

A try-on image is a visual approximation. It does not measure your body, guarantee physical fit or sizing, or ensure the generated garment's color, texture, or appearance matches the real item.

12. Your consent

The interface asks for your consent before sending a personal photograph for processing. You can remove a selected photo before submitting. By using the service, review the final privacy terms provided by the operator; those terms must be completed before production launch.

Privacy details require confirmation before deployment.
